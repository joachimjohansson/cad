The cactus was the first kawaii motif I made that was not a finished idea. It was just a smile in an orange pot. I wanted it to feel like a friend who is in no hurry. It got a simple round body, two small dots and a smile that barely shows.

The problem was the flower. The first cover had a pink flower on top, and it gave the cactus exactly the cheer I was after. But when I tried the motif on small badges, the flower took up too much of the surface and made the face hard to read. I needed a version that could stand on its own.

The decision was to remove the flower completely and let the cactus stand without it. The mint background then did the work, and the cactus became calm rather than insistently sweet. It was a small change in the picture, but it changed how people read it.

I still think the flower belongs on the cover. The version without it found its own life on cards and stickers, where it suits better. It is nice when two versions of the same thing each get a job of their own.
