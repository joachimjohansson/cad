A small game studio had written a short description of a guardian who would stand by a bridge at dusk. They wanted something large and intimidating, but in the first call the director said that players should be able to walk up to the figure without feeling afraid. So we started again from the beginning.

The biggest question was how to show that something is old without making it look worn out. I let moss grow across the head and shoulders, because it gives a calm silhouette and tells the viewer that the figure has been standing there for a long time. The stone got soft edges, as if water had worn it smooth.

The decision I am happiest with is that the eyes only glow. In daylight they are two dark stones. At night they turn blue, and the cracks across the body take on the same colour. The player sees when the guardian wakes up without anyone needing to write it in text.

Last, I drew the turnaround, three views of the same figure, so the animators could check the proportions from every side. It passed review with a few small changes to the moss. In the end the big stone got a name that suited it, and I still think it looks a little sad, even though it is kind.
