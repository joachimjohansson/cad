I started with a cloud on a sticky note in my sketchbook. It was cheerful, had pink cheeks, and looked as if it wanted to be somewhere other than the sky. The note stayed on my desk for several weeks before I knew what the cloud wanted.

The problem was size. Kawaii motifs have to work on everything from a small sticker to a cloth bag, and a face gets too detailed when you zoom out. I reduced the face to three marks: two dots and two pink cheeks. The shape of the cloud was kept simple, almost like a single word.

The decision that made the series work was to let the cloud sit in a mint green teacup. It became a small story about wanting to be at home. The rain picture came later, as a mood. When the cloud is sad, blue drops fall over a light blue background, and when it is home, it sits in the cup with steam curling over the rim.

The series ended with the third picture, the cloud in the cup against a cream background. It became both the cover and the base for the stickers. It is the one I recognise myself in most, because it is quite calm.
