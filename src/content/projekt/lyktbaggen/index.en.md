A game developer wanted a beetle that carries a light through a dark forest. It had to be easy to recognise from far away, so I started with the colour. A violet body gives a calm contrast against the blue and green tones of the night, and the large white eyes help readability.

The hard part was the lantern. It had to read clearly in the picture without lighting up the whole scene. I painted an orange glow under the belly plates and let it spread only a small distance around the body. That way the camera could follow the character without the background turning flat.

Late in the work I decided to make a second version with a weaker glow. It is used when the beetle is tired or hiding, and the player should notice the difference without reading any text. At first I thought it was a small detail, but it did a lot for the mood of the scene.

The project ended with the animators using the glow almost like a clock. When the light dims, the player knows it is almost time to rest. I like it when a picture can do that work on its own.
